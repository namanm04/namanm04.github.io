01 — About

Precision immunology through computation

I am a third-year PhD scholar at IIIT-Delhi, working with Prof. Gajendra P.S. Raghava on machine learning approaches to immunoinformatics. My research focuses on predicting immunologically critical molecular interactions — from proteasomal cleavage sites to antibody-interacting residues.

My recent international visit to the Center for Computational and Theoretical Biology at the University of Würzburg expanded my work to single-cell RNA-Seq analysis using large language models. I build not just models, but publicly accessible web servers that make these tools available to the broader research community.
